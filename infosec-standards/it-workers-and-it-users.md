# IT Workers and IT Users

The relationship between IT workers and IT users is central to the ethical practice of information technology.

## IT Workers

IT workers are responsible for understanding the needs and capabilities of IT users, delivering products and services that meet those needs, and establishing an environment that supports ethical behavior. This includes discouraging software piracy, minimizing inappropriate use of corporate computing resources, and avoiding the inappropriate sharing of information.

## IT Users

IT users rely on IT workers to provide the hardware, software, or services they need, within a specified cost and time frame. Trust runs in both directions: users trust IT workers to act in their best interests, while IT workers trust users to provide relevant information, listen to recommendations, and make wise decisions based on the information provided.

## Establishing an Ethical Environment

Ethical issues can arise in this relationship if IT workers fail to deliver on their promises, recommend products or services for personal gain, or engage in fraudulent activities. Both parties need to communicate effectively, maintain transparency, and uphold ethical standards to keep the relationship positive and productive.

## Takeaway

The relationship between IT workers and IT users is defined by the IT worker's commitment to understanding user needs, delivering tailored solutions, and promoting ethical conduct in IT usage. Upholding these principles is what keeps IT practices within an organization both effective and ethical.
