# IS Auditing: MCQ Practice Questions

Self-test questions covering IS audit process, risk, governance, and outsourcing topics. 

1. Most important factor while evaluating controls is to ensure that the controls:
   a. address the risk
   b. do not reduce productivity
   c. are less costly than the risk
   d. are automated

2. Absence of proper security measures represents a(n):
   a. threat
   b. vulnerability
   c. risk
   d. attack

3. Managing risk down to an acceptable level is the responsibility of:
   a. risk management team
   b. senior business management
   c. the chief information officer
   d. the chief security officer

4. The susceptibility of a business or process to make a material error, assuming no internal controls exist, is:
   a. inherent risk
   b. control risk
   c. detection risk
   d. correction risk

5. An IS auditor developing a risk management program: the FIRST activity to be performed is a(n):
   a. vulnerability assessment
   b. evaluation of controls
   c. identification of assets
   d. gap analysis

6. An IS auditor reviewing an outsourcing contract of IT facilities should be MOST concerned if which clause is missing from the contract:
   a. types of hardware
   b. software configuration
   c. ownership of intellectual property
   d. employee training policy

7. How should the IS auditor proceed if the prior audit report has no workpapers?
   a. Postpone the audit until workpapers are available
   b. Continue the audit while relying on the previous audit report
   c. Attest controls for the highest-risk area
   d. Inform audit management and propose retesting the controls

8. An auditor should hold the closure meeting with the objective of:
   a. discussing audit observations
   b. correcting deficiencies
   c. assessing audit staff performance
   d. presenting the final audit report

9. For communication of audit results, the IS auditor is ultimately responsible to:
   a. legal authorities
   b. senior management/audit committee
   c. the manager of the audited entity
   d. the compliance manager

10. The IT steering committee's role in the IT planning process is to:
    a. document meeting notes
    b. approve expenditure of funds
    c. conduct meetings regularly
    d. approve meeting notes

11. Who is responsible for monitoring the overall project, direction, costs, and schedules for systems-development projects?
    a. IT strategy committee
    b. IT steering committee
    c. senior management
    d. the project team leader

12. An IT steering committee would MOST likely perform which of the following functions?
    a. placement of a purchase order with the approved IT vendor
    b. installation of systems software and application software
    c. monitoring IT project prioritization against business requirements
    d. interviewing staff for the IT department

13. The chairperson for a steering committee who can have a significant impact on a business area would be the:
    a. chief financial officer
    b. chief information officer
    c. project manager
    d. executive-level manager

14. An IS auditor reviewing a critical financial application is concerned about fraud. Which sampling method would BEST assist the auditor?
    a. attribute sampling
    b. variable sampling
    c. discovery sampling
    d. stop-or-go sampling

15. Testing whether the last 50 new user requisitions were correctly processed is an example of:
    a. discovery sampling
    b. substantive testing
    c. compliance testing
    d. stop-or-go sampling

16. With regard to confidence coefficient, it can be said that:
    a. a small sample size gives a high confidence coefficient
    b. if internal controls are known to be strong, the confidence coefficient may be lowered
    c. a small confidence coefficient results in a high sample size
    d. *(this option was cut off in the source document, incomplete as originally written)*

17. An organization has outsourced some of its IS processes. What is the MOST important function for IS management to perform in this scenario?
    a. ensuring outsourcing charges are paid per the SLA
    b. training the outsourced vendor's staff
    c. levying penalties for non-compliance
    d. monitoring the outsourcing provider's performance

18. An organization has outsourced its software development. Which of the following is the responsibility of the organization's own IT management?
    a. ensuring outsourcing charges are paid per the SLA
    b. levying penalties for non-compliance
    c. managing compliance with the contract for the outsourced services
    d. monitoring the outsourced vendor against regulatory requirements

19. An IS auditor observed that outsourcing vendors were appointed without formal written agreements. The IS auditor should recommend that management:
    a. obtain independent assurance of the third-party service providers
    b. set up a process for monitoring the third party's service delivery
    c. ensure formal contracts are put in place
    d. revoke the appointment of the outsourcing vendors
