# Resource-Sharing Models

- **Resource**: anything you might want to access on a network.
- Networks can be categorized by how they allocate resources.
- **Peer-to-peer**: each computer negotiates with all others as an equal. No central administration. Most small LANs today incorporate both peer-to-peer and client-server elements.
- **Client-server**: hosts are divided into clients (request resources) and servers (provide them). Clients request resources from servers.
  - A file server may or may not be a host depending on the context.
  - A printer may or may not be a host depending on the context.
  - A switch is generally not considered a host.
  - A user workstation is typically a host.
